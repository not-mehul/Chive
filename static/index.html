Settings

Trim & fidelity

SponsorBlock · segments

Remove (default) cuts the segments out — chapters and embedded subtitles shift to match. Mark keeps the full video and labels the segments as skippable chapters.

Quality · format preset

1080p MP4 is the default — a balance of fidelity and disk. Audio presets skip video streams entirely.

Library & captions

Embed into file

Plex & Jellyfin. Writes title, uploader, thumbnail and chapter markers into the file so libraries pick them up without sidecars.

Subtitles & captions

Sync. Embedding cuts captions in step with SponsorBlock removal. Sidecar .srt files are not re-timed when segments are cut. Video presets only.

Access & throughput

Cookies · age-restricted videos

Why. YouTube blocks age-restricted videos for signed-out clients. Pointing yt-dlp at a logged-in browser is the simplest fix. The file path is used only when no browser is selected.

Concurrency & archive

Archive. Skips IDs already recorded in archive.txt at the destination root — safe to re-run against the same channel.
